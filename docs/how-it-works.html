How it works

Photos go in; a scaled drawing and a confidence verdict come out.

Stage 1 · Photogrammetry

Structure-from-Motion finds the camera positions; Multi-View Stereo builds a dense surface. The mesh gets its real-world size from printed markers: two markers a known calliper distance apart fix millimetres per unit. Choices for this stage are on the CPU & GPU paths page.

Stage 2 · Cleanup

A raw mesh contains the sherd, turntable, backdrop and floating noise as separate connected pieces. The script keeps the piece with the most triangles. A turntable is a large area built from few triangles; the photographed sherd is a small area built from very many.

Stage 3 · Profile

Why the axis is everything

A wheel-thrown pot is a surface of revolution: every horizontal slice is a circle around one axis. Know the axis and the profile is just "radius at each height". Tilt it by a few degrees and the radius is wrong by an amount that grows with distance. For fragments, finding the axis is the whole problem.

Three ways to find it

MethodIdeaBest for
pcaLongest direction as a seed, then refit through the centres of circles fitted to horizontal slicesComplete or large vessels
rim_arcFit a plane and circle to the rim edge; the plane's normal is the axis. Refuses if the arc is too short, too ragged or not flatRim sherds with enough rim
normals_refine PrototypeEvery surface normal of a surface of revolution meets the axis, so solve for the line that comes closest to all of them; then nudge it until radius depends on height alone. Start from several seeds and check that the good answers agreeSmall or rim-less sherds

Cutting the profile

Every vertex becomes a height along the axis and a distance from it. The height range is split into 300 bands and the median radius in each is the wall. Median rather than maximum, because a handle covers only a minority of the circumference.

Wall thickness

For a sherd that captured both surfaces, each band's radii form two clusters. A one-dimensional Otsu threshold splits them into inner and outer wall; thickness is the gap. An exterior-only mesh is a single cluster and correctly reports none.

Stage 4 · Publish

Plain SVG editing, no GUI: a metric scale bar, a caption and the 0.3 mm line weight. The profile geometry is not touched, and tests check that the lines and scale are identical before and after.

The verdict

SignalMeaningEffect
rim_arc fell backNo clean rim arc of at least 70°REVIEW
normals_refine ambiguousGood axes disagree (sphere or cone-like surface, too little surface)REVIEW
Very low angular coverageUnder a quarter of the circumference present (a tenth for normals_refine)REVIEW
None of theseAxis fixed from real evidenceGO
Honest limit. GO means the method could fix the axis, not that the answer was checked against reality. A short piece of an out-of-round vessel looks like a perfect surface of revolution about a slightly wrong axis, and no fragment-only check can see that. Real-sherd validation against a calliper is what sizes it.
